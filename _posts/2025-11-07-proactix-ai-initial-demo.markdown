## The Phactix AI Story: Closing the Compliance Gap

The modern pharma tech stack—your **CRM and Engagement platforms**—are brilliant at managing **pre-approved content** and tracking planned interactions. They are essential, but they stop at the most vulnerable point: **the human moment.**

### The Unscripted Risk

In the high-stakes meeting between the human rep and the HCP, the conversation is unscripted. When the doctor asks a complex, **off-label question**, the rep faces a massive compliance risk. The old way meant anxiety, interruption, and 20 minutes of manual paperwork after the meeting.

### The Phactix AI Bridge

Phactix AI is the **proactive safety layer** that plugs directly into your existing CRM/CLM ecosystem.

**The Workflow in 60 Seconds:**

1.  **The Question:** HCP asks an off-label question.
2.  **The Intervention:** Phactix AI instantly flags the question and guides the rep (Sarah) with the compliant "bridge language" required to stop the conversation.
3.  **The Handoff:** Sarah assures the doctor the Medical Affairs team will respond.
4.  **The Automation:** Phactix AI takes the verbatim question and **automatically populates and submits the auditable Medical Affairs form.**

We transform the high-risk, unscripted moment into a **low-risk, automated compliance event.** Phactix AI ensures your reps are always protected and free to focus on the human relationship, maximizing the value of every single interaction.
