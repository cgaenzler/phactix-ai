# The Closed-Loop Workflow Advantage: Why Phactix Is New

When you’re talking about AI in the pharmaceutical industry, the conversation often begins and ends with: “Can it answer my question?”

Many tools today are **reactive Q&A systems**, essentially a pharma-focused search engine. An doctor asks a question, the AI provides a compliant answer, and the interaction stops there.

At Phactix, we believe that’s only half the story. The real challenge is **workflow automation**. We don't answer questions; we automate the process that follows, especially when the stakes are highest.

## The Sales Rep's Compliance Dilemma

Imagine you are mid-conversation with a busy physician. They ask a specific, complex question about your therapy that you know is high-risk—a question that requires an official response from Medical Affairs (MA).

In the old way of working, you have two bad options:

1.  **Risk It:** Answer on the spot and potentially risk an off-label compliance violation.
2.  **Lose Momentum:** Say, "I'll get back to you." That means a manual email, hours of waiting for MSL to respond, a delayed follow-up, and a lost connection with the doctor.

That manual handoff—the cycle of forms, emails, and waiting is where crucial time is lost.

## Phactix: Your Instant Compliance Co-Pilot

Phactix AI is designed to be the sales rep’s **instant compliance safety net** a co-pilot in the field that eliminates the compliance risk and the manual burden of follow-up.

Here’s how we transform that high-risk interaction in seconds:

### 1. Real-Time Risk Detection
Phactix is constantly listening and processing locally to detect potential risk. If the doctor asks a question that touches on a sensitive topic—like off-label use—Phactix doesn't try to answer it. Instead, it instantly recognizes the unsolicited question.

### 2. Immediate Guidance
Phactix provides the rep with the compliant way to respond and the Medical Affairs team to provide the compliant answer quickly. This keeps the sales conversation professional and compliant, maintaining trust with the physician.


## The Phactix Vision

We are building a platform that doesn't just make it easier to answer every qustion; it makes it easier to **maintain compliant relationships** with doctors. Our ultimate goal is to become a core infrastructure that enables every pharma sales rep to be compliant, confident, and highly effective in the field.
